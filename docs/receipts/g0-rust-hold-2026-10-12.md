Rebuild after source changes and select one overlay at a time. `hold-test` has a 360-second outer deadline/teardown trap; each call has 30 seconds. Raw evidence remains ignored under `artifacts/`: `g0-hold-*-red.log`, `g0-hold-red.log`, `g0-hold-review-green.log`, `g0-hold-reviewed-build.log`, `g0-hold-pbx-tests.log`, `g0-hold-reviewed-context.log`, `observe-hold-milestone.py` and `g0-hold-final-{hold,calls,controls}-*` matrices/proofs/identities/runtime/observer logs. The public JSON is selected numeric/Boolean evidence, not a configuration/log export.

TLS uses validated SIP signaling with **1301/1302 explicitly plaintext RTP**; mandatory-SRTP 1201/1202 are refused. The incoming Rust update-answer helper is unit-tested, but anchored-PBX runs do not establish its forwarded-update/488 handler execution or dynamic media retargeting. The RTP clock advances within windows, not signaling gaps. Strict single-stream/direction parsing is not general SDP; session/media direction overrides and zero-address hold fail closed. Fixed synthetic RTP initialization, per-window receive tracking, endpoint-wide event watches, upstream parser/channel bounds, adapter/SDK credential copies and incomplete remote cleanup on failure/cancellation remain production gates. No jitter/RTCP/resampling/device/host/VM/NAT/IPv6 or human exact-artifact acceptance is inferred.
